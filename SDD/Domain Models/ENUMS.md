# Domain Enums Specification - Mini-Banckito

This document defines the enumerated types used across the Mini-Banckito domain to control statuses and categorizations.

## Defined Enums:
1. **`AccountType`**: Categorizes bank accounts into `SAVINGS` or `CHECKING`.
2. **`AccountStatus`**: Controls operational state enforcing business rules (`ACTIVE`, `FROZEN`, `CLOSED`).
3. **`TransactionType`**: Classifies transaction entries into `DEPOSIT`, `WITHDRAWAL`, or `TRANSFER`.
